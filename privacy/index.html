Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026 · Last updated: November 1, 2026

LivaChat is a client application for the open-source LivaChat server. This policy explains what the iOS app does with information and what it does not do.

Who we are

LivaChat is developed and published by JIANHUA YANG ("we", "us", "our"). You can contact us at livachat99@gmail.com.

Summary

  • We do not operate a server for the app.
  • We do not collect, receive, or store your personal data through the app.
  • The app connects directly to a LivaChat server that you configure and control.
  • The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Data sent to your server is controlled by you or the operator of that server.

Information the app processes

To provide real-time conversation, the app processes:

  • Microphone audio when you start a voice conversation;
  • Camera video when you enable the camera;
  • Photos you choose when you use the photo picker or save an AI-generated image;
  • Text you enter and AI responses returned by your server;
  • Server address, auth key, device identifier, and app settings needed to connect to your server;
  • Local chat history and generated images stored on your device.

This information is sent directly from the app to the server you configure. It is not sent to us. We do not have access to your server or its data.

Local storage on your device

  • Your server address, auth key, and device identifier are stored locally; the auth key is kept in the iOS Keychain.
  • App settings and chat history are stored on your device.
  • You can delete individual conversations or all local history in the app.
  • Diagnostic logs, if present, remain on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Data on your server

Your LivaChat server may store audio, video, text, transcripts, generated content, and timeline data, depending on how you configure it. You or the server operator decide what is stored, for how long, and who can access it. You can delete server-side data through your server's administration tools.

Permissions

  • Microphone — for real-time voice conversation.
  • Camera — optional video input for multimodal conversation.
  • Photo Library (Add) — to save AI-generated images when you ask the app to do so.
  • Local Network — to connect to a LivaChat server running on your local network.
  • Background Audio — to continue an active voice conversation when the app is backgrounded.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings. The app may not work without the permissions required for the feature you want to use.

Third parties

  • The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Your server may call third-party AI, speech recognition, or speech synthesis providers that you configure. Their privacy policies apply to data sent to them by your server.
  • External links open in Safari or an in-app browser; the websites you visit have their own privacy policies.
  • Apple processes App Store purchases and may provide us with sales and tax reports that do not identify individual buyers.

Support communications

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it to respond to you and to improve support. You can ask us to delete your support correspondence at any time. Please do not send auth keys, passwords, or sensitive personal content unless it is necessary for your request.

Children

LivaChat is intended for adults and is rated 18+ on the App Store. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact us.

Your choices

  • Change or remove your server configuration in the app.
  • Disable microphone or camera access in iOS Settings.
  • Delete local chat history in the app.
  • Delete server-side data through your server's administration tools.
  • Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Data retention

Local data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to respond and meet legal obligations. Server-side data is retained according to your server's configuration.

International users

The app sends data only to the server you configure. We do not transfer your data internationally. If you connect to a server in another country, that server's operator is responsible for the transfer and any applicable laws.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date on this page. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the updated policy.
