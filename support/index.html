Support

LivaChat is a client for a server you run yourself. We do not provide hosting or access to a server. If you need help, start here, then email us.

Contact

Email: livachat99@gmail.com
We aim to respond within 2 business days. Content reports are prioritized and we aim to respond within 24 hours.

Please include your app version, iOS version, server version, and steps to reproduce. Do not send auth keys, passwords, or private conversation content unless it is strictly necessary.

Before you write

  • Make sure your LivaChat server is running and reachable from your device.
  • Check the server address and auth key in the app's Settings.
  • If you use a self-signed certificate, enable "Allow self-signed certificates" in Settings and confirm the certificate is valid for the host.
  • Check that the app has microphone permission in iOS Settings.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need my own server?

Yes. LivaChat is a client for the open-source LivaChat server. You install and run the server on hardware you control. Setup instructions and source code are available at github.com/livachat99.

Do you collect my data?

No. The developer does not operate a server and does not collect, receive, or store your data. Audio, video, and text go directly to the server you configure. See the Privacy Policy.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up or login. You connect the app to your server with its address and auth key.

How do I delete my data?

Delete conversations in the app's History screen. Server-side data, recordings, transcripts, and timeline entries are controlled by your server; use your server's administration tools to delete them.

How do I report AI output or other content?

Use the Report action on an AI message, or email livachat99@gmail.com with the app version, time, and a description. Do not send sensitive personal data unless necessary.

How do refunds work?

App Store purchases and refunds are handled by Apple. Request a refund at support.apple.com/HT204084.

Does the app work without a network connection?

No. The app needs a network connection to your LivaChat server. A server on your local network works while you are on that network.

Reporting content

We have zero tolerance for non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual content involving minors, harassment, threats, and other objectionable content. Report it from the app or by email. We aim to respond within 24 hours and may update the app, documentation, or server guidance as a result.
